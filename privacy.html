1. Who we are

Socio Craftco ("we", "us") is a studio based in Delhi, India that builds websites, online stores, apps and AI agents for businesses. This policy explains how we handle personal data when you visit sociocraftco.in, contact us, or work with us as a client.

For personal data we collect for our own purposes, we are the Data Fiduciary under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act").

2. What we collect

Information you send us

When you use our contact form, message us on WhatsApp, email us or reach us on Instagram or Facebook, we receive what you choose to share. That usually means your name, business name, phone number or email, and details about your project, budget and timeline.

Our contact form doesn't store anything on this website. Submitting it opens WhatsApp with your details filled in, and nothing reaches us until you press send in WhatsApp.

Live demos

The store builder and the WhatsApp AI agent demo run entirely in your browser. What you type into them is not sent to us or stored anywhere. The only exception is when you tap "Get a quote for this store" or "I want an agent like this": that opens WhatsApp with a summary of your choices, which you can review before sending.

Technical data

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers or social media pixels. Our hosting provider may automatically log basic technical information, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to keep the site secure and working. We do not use these logs to identify or profile you.

Client project data

When we build something for you, you may give us access to content, accounts or data about your own customers. We use it only to deliver the work you've asked for, as set out in your project agreement. For your customers' data, we act as your Data Processor and follow your instructions.

3. How we use your data

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a proposal or quote.
  • To deliver, support and invoice the services you've agreed to.
  • To meet our legal obligations, including tax, GST and accounting records.
  • To keep our website and systems secure.

We process your data based on the consent you give when you contact us, or for legitimate uses permitted under the DPDP Act, such as complying with the law. We do not sell your personal data, and we won't send you marketing messages unless you ask us to.

4. Who we share it with

We share personal data only with service providers we need to run our business, and only as much as they need:

  • WhatsApp (Meta), when you message us there.
  • Google, for email and documents.
  • Vercel, which hosts this website.
  • Instagram or Facebook (Meta), if you contact us there.
  • Professional advisers, such as our accountant, under confidentiality.
  • Government or law enforcement authorities, when the law requires it.

Some of these providers may store data on servers outside India. Their own privacy policies also apply to how they handle your data.

5. How long we keep it

If an enquiry doesn't turn into a project, we delete it within 12 months of our last conversation. For clients, we keep project and billing records for as long as Indian tax and accounting laws require, and delete or return project data when it's no longer needed.

6. Security

We take reasonable security measures to protect personal data, including limiting who can access it and using encrypted connections (HTTPS). No system is completely secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

7. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act, you can:

  • Ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it.
  • Ask us to correct, complete or update your data.
  • Ask us to erase your data, unless we must keep it by law.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time. This doesn't affect processing already done.
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf.
  • Raise a grievance with us, and if you're not satisfied, complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

To use any of these rights, email us from the address or phone number you contacted us with, so we can confirm it's you.

8. Children

Our services are meant for businesses and adults. We don't knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent us their data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services or the law change. The effective date at the top always shows the latest version. If a change significantly affects how we use your data, we'll tell our clients directly.

10. Contact and grievances

For questions, requests or complaints about your personal data, contact us at sociocraftco@gmail.com with the subject "Privacy request". We will acknowledge your request promptly and respond within the time required by law.
