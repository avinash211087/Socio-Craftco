Service Delivery Policy

Effective from 11 November 2026

1. No physical shipping

Socio Craftco sells digital services: social media management, advertising management, AI creative, WhatsApp automation and websites. We do not ship any physical products, so no shipping charges apply.

2. How services are delivered

  • Social media content is published or scheduled on your accounts, and shared with you for approval through a shared folder, email or WhatsApp.
  • Ad campaigns run inside your own Meta ad account, which you keep full access to.
  • Reports are sent by email or WhatsApp each month.
  • Websites are delivered live on your domain, with access handed over once the balance is paid.
  • WhatsApp assistants are set up on your WhatsApp Business account.

3. Timelines

Work starts once we receive your payment and the access and materials we need. The start date and delivery schedule for your package or project are confirmed in your proposal or invoice. Monthly deliverables are spread across the paid month.

If we expect a delay on our side, we will tell you in advance. Delays caused by late approvals or materials can move the schedule.

4. Payment confirmation

After you pay online, you receive a receipt by email from Razorpay, and we confirm on WhatsApp or email.

5. Questions

Email sociocraftco@gmail.com or see our Refund & Cancellation Policy.
