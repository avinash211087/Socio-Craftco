Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective from 11 November 2026

1. Setup fees

The one-time setup fee covers onboarding, account setup and planning. It is non-refundable once work on your account has started. If you cancel before we start any work, we refund the setup fee in full.

2. Monthly package fees

Monthly packages are billed monthly in advance and have a 3-month minimum term (6 months for the Clinic Booking System). A monthly fee is non-refundable once that month's service period has started.

After the minimum term, you can cancel at any time with 30 days' written notice by email or WhatsApp. Your service continues until the notice period ends, and no further fees are charged after that.

3. Website projects

Website projects need a 50% advance before work begins and the balance on launch. If a website project is cancelled before launch, we refund the advance minus the value of the work already completed, which we will show you in writing.

4. Ad spend

Advertising budgets are paid by you directly to Meta (Facebook and Instagram) or other ad platforms. We never collect or resell ad spend, so refunds of ad spend are governed by that platform's own policies.

5. How refunds are paid

To request a refund or cancel, email sociocraftco@gmail.com with your invoice number. We reply within 2 weeks. Approved refunds are paid back to the original payment method within 14 working days. Your bank may take a few more days to show the credit.

If a payment was charged twice or by mistake, tell us and we will refund the duplicate amount in full.

6. Questions

See our Terms of Service and Service Delivery Policy, or contact us.
