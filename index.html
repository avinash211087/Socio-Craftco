Paisa

An offline-first money tracker for Indian users. It reads bank SMS directly on the phone, sorts spending into categories automatically and turns it into a clear financial health score. No cloud, so the data never leaves the device.

  • Auto-categorisation that learns from every correction

FAQ

Questions clients usually ask.

Can't find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp and you'll get a direct reply.

How much does a custom website or app cost?

It depends on what it needs to do. A focused business website costs far less than a web app with logins, payments and dashboards. After a short discovery call you get a written, fixed quote, so you know the full cost before anything starts.

How long does a project take?

Smaller websites usually take a few weeks. Apps and AI agents take longer, depending on features and integrations. Your written scope includes a timeline with clear milestones.

Can you build an AI agent that works on WhatsApp?

Yes. We build agents that work on your website and on WhatsApp through the WhatsApp Business Platform, and connect them to your sheets, CRM or booking system. We'll also guide you through Meta's business verification.

Can you work on my existing website or app?

Yes. We can redesign, speed up, fix or add features to an existing product. We review it first and tell you honestly whether to improve it or rebuild it.

What happens after launch?

We handle deployment and handover, and offer ongoing support for hosting, updates, fixes and new features. You're not left alone once the product is live.
