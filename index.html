Personal finance app for Android

  • Android
  • Offline-first

In development

Focusly

Study timer for iPhone

"Put your phone down. Get your future up." Students tap Start focus and place their phone face down on the desk. While it stays down, Focusly counts focused time upward. Pick it up and the count pauses on its own, with no pause button and no session length to choose.

  • Face-down timer using the phone's motion sensors, with a 3·2·1 settle and adjustable sensitivity
  • Study timetable with Google Calendar sync and a reminder before every block
  • Daily goal ring, streaks, weekly analytics and a focus heatmap
  • iOS
  • Motion sensors
  • Live Activities
  • Google Calendar API

FAQ

Questions clients usually ask.

Can't find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp for a direct reply.

How much does a custom website or app cost?

It depends on what it needs to do. A focused business website costs far less than an app with logins, payments and dashboards. After a short discovery call you get a written, fixed quote, so you know the full cost before anything starts.

How long does a project take?

Smaller websites usually take a few weeks. Stores, apps and AI agents take longer depending on features and integrations. Your written scope includes a timeline with clear milestones.

Is the WhatsApp AI agent on this page real?

The demo uses scripted replies so you can see the flow. A real agent is trained on your services, prices and FAQs, runs on the WhatsApp Business Platform, and sends leads to your WhatsApp, Google Sheet or CRM.

Can you work on my existing website or app?

Yes. We can redesign, speed up, fix or add features to an existing product. We review it first and tell you honestly whether to improve it or rebuild it.

What happens after launch?

We handle deployment and handover, and offer ongoing support for hosting, updates, fixes and new features.
